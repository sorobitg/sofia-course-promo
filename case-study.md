# Sofía Course Promo — Case Study

**Client:** Sofía (Digital Course Creator)
**Project:** Animated Social Media Campaign — *Master Scroll-Stopping Graphics*
**Deliverable:** 15-Second Reel + Landing Page + Asset Package

---

## Overview

A character-driven animated campaign designed to increase engagement and conversions for a digital course on social media graphics. The campaign centres on Sofía, a friendly illustrated character who guides viewers through a problem-agitate-solution narrative compressed into a 15-second vertical reel, optimised for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.

---

## The Challenge

Course creators in the social media education space consistently struggle with two compounding problems:

1. **Low organic reach** — generic, static promotional posts fail to stop the scroll and are buried by platform algorithms.
2. **High bounce rate** — even when users do click through, landing pages lack the visual storytelling needed to convert cold traffic into enrolments.

Sofía's existing promotional assets were text-heavy, lacked a consistent visual identity, and did not reflect the high-quality teaching experience inside the course.

---

## The Solution

We created a **scroll-stopping visual system** built around three pillars:

### 1 — Character-Driven Storytelling
A single illustrated character (Sofía) appears in every slide, building instant recognition and emotional connection. She transitions through relatable micro-expressions — thinking, pointing, presenting — that mirror the viewer's own journey from problem to solution.

### 2 — Proven Content Architecture
The 15-second reel follows a battle-tested narrative arc:

| Phase | Slides | Goal |
|-------|--------|------|
| Hook | 01–02 | Stop the scroll; surface the pain point |
| Value | 03–06 | Deliver concrete, credible promises |
| Close | 07–09 | Build desire; drive to CTA |

### 3 — Short-Form Animation Optimised for Algorithms
- **1080×1920 vertical format** — native to Reels, TikTok, and Shorts
- **30fps** — smooth motion with minimal file size
- **On-screen text** — every message readable with sound off
- **Bold colour transitions** — each slide uses a distinct visual cue to maintain momentum

## Results (Expected)

Based on comparable character-animated campaigns in the online education vertical:

- **Higher watch time** — character continuity keeps viewers engaged past the 3-second mark, the critical threshold for algorithmic boost
- **More engagement** — relatable pain-point hook generates comments and shares from creators who recognise themselves in slide 02
- **Increased conversions** — social-proof statistics (89% engagement lift, +215% growth) combined with a clear CTA in the final slide reduce decision friction
- **Stronger brand recognition** — the Sofía character becomes a recognisable brand asset reusable across emails, thumbnails, and course materials
